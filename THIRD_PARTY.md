The generated STM32F7 HAL and CMSIS material is retained only as target build infrastructure for the STM32F767 example. Product teams should obtain and retain the official STM32CubeF7 package through their approved supply-chain process and assess every dependency according to their software supply-chain process. The standalone UDS/ISO-TP library has no CANopen dependency.
